## Site selection, zoning & land use
- **Loudoun County Phase 2 draft due this month** — county staff are scheduled to present a Phase 2 draft (new use-specific standards for data centers and utility substations) to the Board of Supervisors in May 2026, with Planning Commission public comment in July and target adoption by December. Phase 1 (March 2025) already removed by-right zoning for data centers. ([Loudoun County](https://www.loudoun.gov/6222/Phase-2-Data-Center-Standards-Locations), [Loudoun Now](https://www.loudounnow.com/news/work-begins-on-next-phase-of-loudoun-county-data-center-regulations/article_d9a0c8fa-3ea6-4a13-814e-83a62820d06d.html))
- **Prince William rezoning back to Va. Supreme Court** — developer is appealing the April 2026 Va. Court of Appeals decision that blocked the rezoning for the proposed Manassas-area data center corridor. Worth watching for how the court treats the comp-plan and special-exception analysis. ([Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/may/4/developer-asks-virginia-supreme-court-overturn-ruling-blocking-data/))
- **State-level zoning preemption fights** — multiple state legislatures are running bills that would either preempt local zoning to favor data centers or, conversely, give localities more denial authority. Tracker piece is a useful one-stop. ([MultiState](https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/1/15/state-data-center-legislation-faces-local-zoning-battles))

## Power, utility & grid
- **PA PUC ratepayer-protection measure advances** — order frames cost-causation as the default: large loads pay for the transmission/distribution they require. Expect copycat dockets in MD, NJ, OH. ([WVIA](https://www.wvia.org/news/local/2026-05-04/pa-public-utility-commission-advances-measure-that-aims-to-protect-ratepayers-from-data-center-demand))
- **Meta–MN8 80 MW solar PPA (Walker Solar, PJM)** — adds renewable capacity inside PJM ahead of end-of-2026 COD; useful comp for U.S. PPA pricing in PJM. ([ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/meta-signs-solar-ppa-with-mn8-energy-to-power-u-s-data-centers/), [Carbon Credits](https://carboncredits.com/meta-strikes-80-mw-solar-deal-to-power-data-centers-and-cut-carbon-impact/))
- **TotalEnergies–Google 15-yr PPA, Ohio** — 1.5 TWh/yr from Montpelier solar farm, supporting Google's Ohio campuses. ([APPA](https://www.publicpower.org/periodical/article/totalenergies-supply-google-data-center-operations-under-renewable-energy-pa))
- **Interconnection queue still the binding constraint** — 2.3 TW sitting in queues, median wait >4 years; Google's first owned "Energy Park" went live in March 2026 by sidestepping a five-year PJM queue. Behind-the-meter and developer-owned generation is the trend to budget for in deal docs. ([Pillsbury](https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/power-purchase-interconnection-agreements-data-centers.html))
- **Illinois AG objection at FERC on ComEd data center agreements** — challenges the cost allocation methodology; precedent risk for similar bilateral arrangements. ([Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/illinois-ag-files-objections-to-comed-data-center-agreements-at-ferc/809576/))

Diligence any IL-sited project for grandfathering and "deemed-new" risk. ([Stateline](https://stateline.org/2026/02/24/data-center-tax-breaks-are-on-the-chopping-block-in-some-states/))

([Stateline](https://stateline.org/2026/02/24/data-center-tax-breaks-are-on-the-chopping-block-in-some-states/))
- **Texas** — comptroller program continues, but $3.2B two-year revenue impact is fueling sunset/reform talk. ([Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/04/08/texas-data-centers-sales-tax-break-billion-dollars/), [Texas Comptroller](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/data-centers/))

## Regulation & litigation
- **FERC large-load interconnection rulemaking — decision by end of June 2026** — will reshape how new data center loads queue and pay. DOE has separately directed FERC to accelerate interconnection. Key items to track: cost allocation, prudency standard for behind-the-meter, and whether co-located gen-plus-load gets a streamlined path. ([Pillsbury](https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/ferc-regulation-data-centers.html), [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/doe-directs-ferc-accelerate-interconnection-data-centers), [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/industry-participants-anticipate-ferc-ruling-could-alter-regulatory-framework-data))
- **WilmerHale flags rising state-law litigation risk** — state-specific burdens (rate adders, water surcharges, environmental review novelties) will draw legal challenges under preemption, dormant Commerce Clause, and takings theories. Worth a read for issue-spotting current deals. ([WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20260223-state-regulation-of-data-centers-emerging-trends-and-potential-legal-complexities))
- **Noise / pollution litigation pipeline forming** — plaintiff-side firms are openly marketing data center noise and emissions cases; expect more nuisance and property-damage filings near operating campuses in 2026–2027. ([Weitz & Luxenberg](https://www.weitzlux.com/environmental/pollution/data-center-pollution-litigation/))
