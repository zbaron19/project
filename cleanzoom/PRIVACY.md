# CleanZoom Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-20_

CleanZoom is designed around a single promise: **your browsing data never
leaves your browser.**

## What CleanZoom does

CleanZoom shows a larger preview of images you hover on the web pages you
already visit. All of this happens locally, inside your browser, on the page
you are already looking at.

## Data we collect

**None.** CleanZoom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information or browsing activity. Specifically:

- It makes **no network requests of its own**. It does not contact any server
  operated by us or any third party. (When it displays a preview, your browser
  loads that image from the same website you are already viewing — exactly as
  the page itself would.)
- It contains **no analytics, telemetry, trackers, or advertising**.
- It does **not** read, store, or transmit page text, form data, passwords,
  cookies, or browsing history.

## Data we store

CleanZoom saves only **your own settings** (hover delay, activation key,
maximum preview size, and the list of sites you disabled). These are stored
using Chrome's `storage.sync` API, which keeps them in your browser/Google
account. We never receive a copy of them.

## Permissions

- `storage` — to remember your settings.
- Host access (content script) — required to display previews on the pages you
  visit. It is used solely to detect hovered images and render the preview; no
  page content is recorded or sent anywhere.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the support email listed on the
extension's Chrome Web Store page.
