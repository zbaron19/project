How it works

Three turns of a very old wheel

Pick a question worth answering honestly. Speak plainly, even messily. Be heard — and kept.

It asks, never answers

One real question at a time, built from the exact words you just used. No advice, no lectures, no “you should.” It draws your thinking out instead of pouring its own in.

It keeps the record

Every session is saved — in your words, on your device. The archive becomes the one place your interior life is written down and searchable, year over year.

It shows you the long view

Sit with a few questions and Examined reads across everything you've said and writes you a letter — the thread you keep returning to, where your thinking shifted, the question you never quite land.

It is comfortable with “I don't know.” It treats that as the doorway, not the wall.

What it is, and isn't

Be clear about the thing

It's easy to mistake this for four other products. It's none of them.

✕Not a chatbot. Those exist to answer. This one only asks.
✕Not a journal. A blank page never asks the second question.
✕Not therapy or coaching. No advice, no diagnosis, no “should.”
✕Not an AI companion. No fake intimacy, no streak, no pull to come back.
✓A Socratic thinking partner with infinite patience.
✓That remembers — and reflects your own mind back to you.
