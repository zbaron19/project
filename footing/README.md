# Footing

**A private, judgment-free companion for rebuilding your financial life after addiction.**

Open `index.html` in any browser — phone or laptop. No install, no account, no
network. Everything you enter stays in that browser, on that device.

---

## What it is

Footing turns the most frightening, shame-soaked part of recovery — the money —
into something you can look at without flinching. It does for *financial*
recovery what a sobriety tracker does for time:

- **The Inventory** — a fearless, private list of everything owed: cards, loans,
  back taxes, judgments, and the people you mean to make whole.
- **The Plan** — one monthly number you can actually keep, and an order of
  repayment (people first / smallest first / costliest first). It projects your
  real debt-free date, month by month, with interest.
- **Milestones** — financial recovery deserves the same chips that time does:
  *faced the number, first payment, first person made whole, halfway back,
  debt-free.*
- **Today** — a calm daily check-in and three streaks: *day streak, days on
  plan, days rebuilding.* A slip is information, not a verdict.
- **Support & guardrails** — links to the GA Pressure Relief process, nonprofit
  credit counseling, your rights with collectors, and 988. Plus one-tap backup
  export/import, because the data is yours.

It is explicitly **not** financial, legal, or therapeutic advice, and it never
touches your accounts. It's an organizing and motivation tool — a place to be
honest in private and watch yourself climb back.

## The market

- ~2M+ U.S. adults meet criteria for gambling disorder; ~7M+ for problem
  gambling — and that's one addiction. Financial wreckage is near-universal
  across alcohol and substance recovery too.
- The wreckage is the #1 relapse trigger and the #1 thing recovery programs are
  *least* equipped to help with. Treatment handles the using; almost no one
  handles the rebuilding.
- Financial-wellness tooling is a crowded category — but nothing is built
  *for recovery*, in recovery's language, with recovery's dignity. That framing
  is the whole product.

## Ways it pays (the $300–400K path, grounded in your own notes)

Your `cre-title-finance-300k-income-path` note wanted leverage, flexible hours,
and income beyond your own hours. Three stacking engines:

1. **B2C subscription** — a calm $8–12/mo companion. 3,000 paying members ≈
   $300K+/yr. Recovery has the strongest word-of-mouth distribution of any
   community on earth (the fellowships *are* a referral network).
2. **B2B / B2B2C** — license to treatment centers, IOPs, EAPs, and financial
   counselors as the "financial recovery" module they currently lack. A handful
   of $1–3K/mo contracts is real revenue with almost no marginal cost.
3. **Productized financial-amends service** — your own note's "Path 3."
   A fixed-fee engagement: inventory + repayment plan + creditor-communication
   templates, delivered with the app, junior talent doing intake. Leverage,
   not hours.

The app is the wedge; the expertise is the margin.

## Honest risks

- **Sensitivity & liability.** Money + addiction is a vulnerable space. The
  not-advice line must stay bright; partner with licensed counselors for
  anything that crosses into advice.
- **Trust is the product.** Privacy-first/on-device isn't a feature here, it's
  the entire reason someone would type their worst number into a box. Never
  break that.
- **You are the credibility.** This wants a face and a story. That's a personal
  ask — but it's also the moat no competitor can copy.

## Stack

One self-contained `index.html`. Vanilla JS, `localStorage`, zero dependencies,
zero network calls — same instinct that made the recovery tracker a single
static page. Easy to host anywhere (or nowhere), trivial to wrap in the
iOS shell you already know how to ship.
