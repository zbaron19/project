An honest note

Footing is a private organizing and motivation tool. It is not financial, legal, tax, or therapeutic advice, and it does not talk to your banks. For real decisions — bankruptcy, settlements, tax debt, judgments — talk to a qualified professional and to people in your program. Recovery is the foundation; the money is what you rebuild on top of it. One day at a time.
