- **[political-2] Review pending state legislation touching data centers (tariffs, incentives, siting, water, noise)** — Two bills carried over in the legislature touching the data center exemption (performance reporting; ratepayer-protection conditions on large-load tariffs). Neither passed; both expected back next session.

## Counsel's recommendation

Proceed to PSA, with closing conditioned on (1) recordation of an amended, corridor-confined transmission easement resolving title-1 — this is the single go/no-go item; (2) a written will-serve letter superseding the study-queue acknowledgment (power-2); and (3) rollback-tax allocation to seller (title-5). The watch items are manageable in the ordinary course; the wildlife survey window (environmental-3) should drive the grading schedule now, not at permit time.
