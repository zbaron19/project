▍The problem

Data center land is trading at numbers that made no sense three years ago — and the thing that makes a parcel worth $2M an acre instead of $40K isn't visible from the listing. It's a will-serve letter that doesn't say what the broker says it says. A zoning ordinance amended last quarter. A water right that won't transfer. A sales tax exemption with a sunset nobody read. A moratorium that's three council votes away.

The buyers moving fastest in this market are spending lightest on the question that matters most: is the megawatt story real, and what breaks it?

Most failed data center deals don't die at closing. They die eighteen months later, in a public hearing, with the land already owned.
