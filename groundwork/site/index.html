▍Why a brief about dirt

Data center land in the Columbia Basin is trading at numbers that made no sense three years ago — and the thing that separates a $2M/acre parcel from a $40K/acre farm field isn't visible from the listing. It's a will-serve letter that doesn't say what the broker says it says. A zoning ordinance amended last quarter. A water right that won't transfer. A tax exemption with a jobs covenant nobody modeled. A moratorium that's three council votes away in the next county over.

The Groundwork Brief reads the utility dockets, county agendas, session bills, and industry press so the people buying and advising on this land start the week already oriented.

Most failed data center deals don't die at closing. They die eighteen months later, in a public hearing, with the land already owned.
