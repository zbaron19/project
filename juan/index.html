Catering, allegedly

The California Burrito

Carne asada. Fries. Inside.

Invented in San Diego and never improved upon: carne asada, French fries, cheese, and guacamole in a flour tortilla. The fries are not a side — they are a structural component. One per day, minimum. This is the floor, not the ceiling.

House rule: rice in a California burrito is an automatic disqualification. We didn't make the rules. San Diego did.

  • 3+Runs scheduled
  • ~700gPer unit
  • 0gRice tolerated
  • 0Regrets
Title match · Sat 2:30 PM

K1 Speed

Three heats. One belt. A lifetime of trash talk.

20-horsepower electric karts on a barrier-lined indoor circuit. A real race format: qualifying heat to set the grid, a points heat, and a winner-takes-the-belt championship final — inverted grid, so the fast guys have to fight through traffic like a royal rumble on wheels.

Timing is transponder-based and printed, so when your kart was "definitely slower," we'll have the data.

  • 3Heats
  • 45MPH top speed
  • ~50Total laps
  • 1Belt
Nightcap · Sat evening

Padres Under the Lights

From the title match straight to Petco Park. Best ballpark in baseball, twenty minutes from the podium, sunset over downtown — and yes, the belt is coming to the game. Ballpark food does not count against the burrito program. A Saints man can respect brown and gold for one night.
