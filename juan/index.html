Fuel program

The California Burrito

Carne asada. Fries. Inside.

Invented in San Diego and never improved upon: carne asada, French fries, cheese, and guacamole in a flour tortilla. The fries are not a side — they are a structural component. We will be eating one per day, minimum. This is the floor, not the ceiling.

House rule: rice in a California burrito is a disqualifying offense. We didn't make the rules. San Diego did.

  • 3+Runs scheduled
  • ~700gPer unit
  • 0gRice tolerated
  • 0Regrets
Main event · Sat 2:30 PM

K1 Speed

Three heats. One champion. A lifetime of trash talk.

20-horsepower electric karts on a barrier-lined indoor circuit. We run a real race format: a qualifying heat to set the grid, a points heat, and a winner-takes-everything Grand Prix final — inverted grid, so the fast guys have to earn it through traffic.

Timing is transponder-based and printed, so when your kart was "definitely slower," we'll have the data.

  • 3Heats
  • 45MPH top speed
  • ~50Total laps
  • 1Trophy
Nightcap · Sat evening
