Item 1 — California Burrito

A California burrito is a flour tortilla containing carne asada, French fries, cheese, and guacamole. It originated in San Diego and is not improved by additions. The specification below is normative; deviations require a change request under Section 9.2.

3.5 — Handling & consumption procedure

Consume from the folded end. The foil sleeve shall be retracted in increments not exceeding 40 mm; full removal at commencement is the leading cause of Class II seam failure. Maintain the unit at 15–25° above horizontal throughout. Do not set the unit down. Do not photograph the unit before the second bite — the section face is not presentable until then.

3.6 Moisture hazard Pico de gallo migrates downward under gravity and pools at the terminal fold, producing the failure mode known internally as "the last three inches." Attendees electing pico accept this risk. No mitigation is available. Napkin allocation is four per attendee, held by Dave.
