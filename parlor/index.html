    How to Play

    Someone was murdered last night. Four guests, four items, four rooms — each guest carried exactly one item and stayed in exactly one room.

    Read the testimony, then fill in the three deduction grids. A ● means a pairing is certain; a ✕ means it's impossible. Every puzzle has exactly one logical solution — no guessing needed.

    The coroner tells you which room the murder happened in. Work out who was in that room, and what they were carrying.

    You get three accusations. Choose wisely, detective.
