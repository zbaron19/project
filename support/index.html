Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with GameGuru?

Download GameGuru from the App Store, create an account, and start exploring game guides, tips, and strategies right away.

Is GameGuru free to use?

Yes, GameGuru is free to download and use. Some premium content and features may be available through in-app purchases.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings within the app and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID settings. Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device to view, change, or cancel your subscription.

I'm having trouble with the app. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app, or restarting your device. If the issue persists, make sure you have the latest version installed from the App Store. If you still need help, contact us at support@gameguruapp.com.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion by contacting us at support@gameguruapp.com. We will process your request and delete all associated data within 30 days.
