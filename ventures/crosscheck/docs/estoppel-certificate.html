Tenant Estoppel Certificate

TO: Harborline Acquisitions LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, and its lender, together with their respective successors and assigns (collectively, the “Reliance Parties”)

RE: Suite 400, 1100 Dexter Avenue North, Seattle, Washington 98109 (the “Premises”)

The undersigned, MERIDIAN ANALYTICS, INC., a Delaware corporation (“Tenant”), understands that Harborline Acquisitions LLC intends to acquire the building of which the Premises are a part from Dexter Lakeview Properties LLC (“Landlord”), and that the Reliance Parties will rely on the certifications in this Certificate. Tenant hereby certifies to the Reliance Parties, as of March 16, 2026, as follows:

1. Tenant is the tenant under that certain Office Lease dated March 1, 2019 between Landlord and Tenant (the “Lease”) covering the Premises, consisting of approximately 12,125 rentable square feet.

2. The Lease has not been amended, modified, or supplemented except by the Commencement Date Memorandum dated July 10, 2019 and the First Amendment to Office Lease dated September 15, 2021. The documents described in this Paragraph 2 constitute the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant with respect to the Premises. True, correct, and complete copies of such documents have been delivered to the Reliance Parties.

3. The Commencement Date of the Lease was June 1, 2019. The Term of the Lease expires on May 31, 2028.

4. The current monthly Base Rent payable under the Lease is $28,955.77. Tenant is also obligated to pay its proportionate share of operating expenses, taxes, and insurance as additional rent in accordance with the Lease.

5. Base Rent and all additional rent have been paid through March 31, 2026. No rent has been paid more than one (1) month in advance.

6. Landlord holds a security deposit under the Lease in the amount of $48,500.00. Tenant has not received, and does not claim, any credit against the security deposit.

7. Tenant has one (1) remaining option to extend the term of the Lease for a period of five (5) years, on the terms stated in the Lease. Tenant has not exercised such option as of the date of this Certificate. Tenant has no other options, rights of first refusal, or rights of first offer with respect to the Premises or the Building.

8. To Tenant’s actual knowledge, neither Landlord nor Tenant is in default under the Lease, and no event has occurred that, with the giving of notice or the passage of time, would constitute such a default.

9. To Tenant’s actual knowledge, Tenant has no existing defenses, offsets, claims, or credits against the payment of rent or the enforcement of the Lease. Tenant is in occupancy of the entire Premises and is operating its business therein.

10. The individual executing this Certificate on behalf of Tenant is duly authorized to do so. This Certificate is given with the understanding that the Reliance Parties will rely upon it in connection with the acquisition and financing of the Building.

EXECUTED as of March 16, 2026.

TENANT: Meridian Analytics, Inc.


By: D. Reyes, Vice President, Finance
