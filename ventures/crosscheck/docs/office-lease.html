Office Lease

THIS OFFICE LEASE (this “Lease”) is made as of March 1, 2019 (the “Effective Date”), by and between DEXTER LAKEVIEW PROPERTIES LLC, a Washington limited liability company (“Landlord”), and MERIDIAN ANALYTICS, INC., a Delaware corporation (“Tenant”).

1. PREMISES.

1.1 Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, those certain premises commonly known as Suite 400 of the building located at 1100 Dexter Avenue North, Seattle, Washington 98109 (the “Building”), consisting of approximately 12,125 rentable square feet as depicted on Exhibit A (the “Premises”).

2. DEFINITIONS.

2.1 Capitalized terms used and not otherwise defined herein have the meanings given in the Basic Lease Information or elsewhere in this Lease. “Lease Year” means each successive twelve (12) month period commencing on the Commencement Date or an anniversary thereof.

3. TERM.

3.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Lease (the “Term”) shall be eighty-four (84) months, commencing on the Commencement Date and expiring on the last day of the eighty-fourth (84th) full calendar month thereafter (the “Expiration Date”), subject to confirmation in the Commencement Date Memorandum to be executed by the parties pursuant to Section 3.3. The anticipated Commencement Date is June 1, 2019.

3.2 Option to Extend. Tenant shall have one (1) option to extend the Term for a period of five (5) years (the “Renewal Term”) at the Fair Market Rate determined pursuant to Exhibit B, exercisable only by written notice delivered to Landlord no later than nine (9) months prior to the expiration of the Term, time being of the essence. Tenant’s option shall be void if Tenant is in default beyond applicable cure periods at the time of exercise or at the commencement of the Renewal Term, or if Tenant has assigned this Lease other than to a Permitted Transferee.

3.3 Commencement Date Memorandum. Promptly following the Commencement Date, Landlord and Tenant shall execute a memorandum confirming the Commencement Date and the Expiration Date, substantially in the form of Exhibit D.

6. OPERATING EXPENSES; TRIPLE NET.

6.1 Net Lease. This Lease is intended to be a triple net lease. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay Tenant’s Proportionate Share (stipulated to be 22.5%) of all Operating Expenses, Real Property Taxes, and insurance costs for the Building, as additional rent (“Additional Rent”), in monthly estimated installments subject to annual reconciliation.

6.2 Operating Expenses. “Operating Expenses” means all costs of ownership, operation, management, maintenance, and repair of the Building and common areas, including common area maintenance charges, subject to the exclusions set forth on Exhibit C. “Controllable Operating Expenses” means all Operating Expenses other than Real Property Taxes, insurance premiums, utilities, and costs of compliance with laws first enacted after the Effective Date.

6.3 Cap on Controllable Operating Expenses. Notwithstanding Section 6.1, for purposes of computing Tenant’s Proportionate Share, Controllable Operating Expenses shall not increase by more than four percent (4%) per calendar year over the amount of Controllable Operating Expenses for the immediately preceding calendar year, determined on a cumulative and compounding basis.

6.4 Audit Right. Tenant may, within one hundred eighty (180) days after receipt of Landlord’s annual reconciliation statement, audit Landlord’s books and records relating to Operating Expenses for the applicable year, on not less than ten (10) business days’ notice.

7. SECURITY DEPOSIT.

7.1 Deposit. Concurrently with Tenant’s execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of Forty-Eight Thousand Five Hundred and No/100 Dollars ($48,500.00) (the “Security Deposit”) as security for Tenant’s faithful performance of its obligations under this Lease. The Security Deposit is not an advance payment of Rent or a measure of Landlord’s damages. Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the later of the Expiration Date and Tenant’s surrender of the Premises in the condition required by this Lease.

8. USE.

8.1 Tenant shall use the Premises solely for general office use and for no other purpose without Landlord’s prior written consent.

9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS.

9.1 Landlord shall maintain the structural elements, roof, and Building systems serving the Building generally; Tenant shall, at its expense, maintain the interior, non-structural portions of the Premises in good order and repair.

10. ALTERATIONS.

10.1 Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises costing more than $25,000 in any instance, or affecting the Building structure or systems, without Landlord’s prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING.

11.1 Consent Required. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any portion of the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Tenant may, without Landlord’s consent but upon prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to a Permitted Transferee, meaning an entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control with Tenant, or the successor by merger or acquisition of substantially all of Tenant’s assets, provided the transferee’s tangible net worth is not less than Tenant’s as of the Effective Date. Fifty percent (50%) of any transfer premium shall be paid to Landlord.

12. DEFAULT; REMEDIES.

12.1 The failure to pay Rent within five (5) business days after written notice, or the failure to perform any other covenant within thirty (30) days after written notice (subject to extension for cures diligently pursued), shall constitute an event of default, upon which Landlord may exercise all remedies available at law or in equity, including termination of this Lease.

13. MISCELLANEOUS.

13.1 This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington. This Lease, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement of the parties with respect to the Premises and may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

EXHIBIT C — OPERATING EXPENSE EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

1. Operating Expenses shall exclude: costs of capital improvements except to the extent amortized over useful life; ground rent; debt service; leasing commissions and tenant-improvement costs for other tenants; costs reimbursed by insurance or other tenants; and Landlord’s general corporate overhead.

2. Management fees included in Operating Expenses shall not exceed three percent (3%) of gross revenues of the Building.

3. Cap. Notwithstanding anything in the Lease to the contrary, increases in Controllable Operating Expenses shall be capped at five percent (5%) per calendar year on a cumulative and compounding basis.

4. Landlord shall operate the Building in a commercially reasonable manner consistent with comparable Class A office buildings in the Lake Union submarket.
