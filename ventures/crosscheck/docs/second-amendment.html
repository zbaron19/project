Second Amendment to Office Lease

This Second Amendment to Office Lease (this “Second Amendment”) is entered into as of November 1, 2023, by and between DEXTER LAKEVIEW PROPERTIES LLC, a Washington limited liability company (“Landlord”), and MERIDIAN ANALYTICS, INC., a Delaware corporation (“Tenant”).

RECITALS

A. Landlord and Tenant are parties to that certain Office Lease dated March 1, 2019, as confirmed by that certain Commencement Date Memorandum dated July 10, 2019, and as amended by that certain First Amendment to Office Lease dated September 15, 2021 (collectively, the “Lease”), for Suite 400 at 1100 Dexter Avenue North, Seattle, Washington (the “Premises”).

B. In light of current market conditions and in consideration of Tenant’s continued occupancy through the extended Term, the parties desire to adjust the Base Rent schedule and the Security Deposit, on the terms set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Defined Terms. Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Second Amendment have the meanings given in the Lease.

2. Recitals. The foregoing recitals are incorporated into this Second Amendment.

3. Base Rent. Effective as of January 1, 2024, the Base Rent schedule set forth in Section 4 of the First Amendment is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

PeriodMonthly Base Rent
01/01/2024 – 05/31/2024$26,000.00

Base Rent for periods prior to January 1, 2024 shall remain as set forth in the First Amendment, and no retroactive adjustment shall be made.

4. Security Deposit. The Security Deposit held by Landlord under Section 7.1 of the Lease is hereby reduced from $48,500.00 to Forty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($40,000.00). Landlord shall credit the difference of $8,500.00 against the installment of Base Rent due January 1, 2024. From and after the date hereof, all references in the Lease to the Security Deposit shall mean $40,000.00.

5. No Other Changes to Economic Terms. Tenant’s Proportionate Share, the cap on Controllable Operating Expenses, and Tenant’s option to extend under Section 3.2 of the Lease (as affected by Section 5 of the First Amendment) remain unmodified by this Second Amendment.

6. Brokers. Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Second Amendment.

7. Ratification. Except as expressly amended hereby, the Lease remains unmodified and in full force and effect and is hereby ratified and confirmed. In the event of a conflict between this Second Amendment and the Lease, this Second Amendment shall control.
