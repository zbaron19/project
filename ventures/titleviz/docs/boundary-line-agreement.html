THIS BOUNDARY LINE AGREEMENT is made by and between Theodore J. Aldercroft and Susan M. Aldercroft, husband and wife, owners of Lot 4, Block 2, Cedarbrook Heights Division No. 3 ("Lot 4"), and Chidi N. Okonkwo and Amara E. Okonkwo, a married couple, owners of Lot 5, Block 2, Cedarbrook Heights Division No. 3 ("Lot 5"), both according to the plat thereof recorded in Volume 89 of Plats, pages 45 through 47, records of King County, Washington.

RECITALS: A cedar fence has existed between Lot 4 and Lot 5 for in excess of twenty years. A survey commissioned by the parties in November 2023 disclosed that the fence line deviates from the platted common boundary by up to 1.8 feet in favor of Lot 4 at the rear corner. The parties desire to resolve any uncertainty as to the location of the common boundary without litigation, pursuant to the procedures available under chapter 58.04 RCW.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereby fix and agree that the common boundary between Lot 4 and Lot 5, Block 2, Cedarbrook Heights Division No. 3, shall be the line of the existing cedar fence as described by metes and bounds on Exhibit A attached hereto and depicted on the Record of Survey referenced therein, which line shall constitute the true and agreed common boundary between said lots and shall be binding upon the parties, their heirs, successors, and assigns, and shall run with the land.

Each party releases and quitclaims to the other any interest in land lying on the other party's side of the agreed boundary line. This Agreement does not create a new parcel and is entered in accordance with RCW 58.04.007.
