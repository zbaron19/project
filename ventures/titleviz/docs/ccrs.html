Recitals

A. The original Declaration of Restrictions for Cedarbrook Heights Division No. 3 was recorded June 14, 1962, under Recording No. 5432110, records of King County, Washington.

B. The Members of the Association have approved this Amended and Restated Declaration, which supersedes and replaces the original Declaration in its entirety as to all Lots.

C. Certain provisions of the original Declaration purported to restrict ownership or occupancy on the basis of race or national origin. Such provisions are void under RCW 49.60.224 and are not restated herein. Any Owner may record a restrictive covenant modification document striking such void provisions from the record pursuant to RCW 49.60.227. Nothing in this Declaration shall be construed to revive or preserve any such void provision.

Article IV — Assessments

Section 4.1. Creation of the Lien and Personal Obligation of Assessments. Each Owner of any Lot, by acceptance of a deed therefor, whether or not it shall be so expressed in such deed, is deemed to covenant and agree to pay to the Association annual assessments and special assessments for capital improvements, such assessments to be established and collected as hereinafter provided. Each such assessment, together with interest, costs, and reasonable attorneys' fees, shall be a charge on the land and shall be a continuing lien upon the Lot against which each such assessment is made. The lien may be foreclosed by the Association in like manner as a mortgage on real property.

Article V — Use Restrictions (excerpt)

Section 5.1. Residential Use. All Lots shall be used for single-family residential purposes only. Section 5.3. Structures. No structure shall be erected or maintained on any Lot other than one detached single-family dwelling and customary accessory structures. Section 5.7. Fences. No fence exceeding six (6) feet in height shall be erected on any Lot; no fence shall be erected within the front-yard setback shown on the face of the plat.
