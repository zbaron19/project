King County, Washington

Dedication

KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS that Cedarbrook Development Company, a Washington corporation, owner in fee simple of the land hereby platted, hereby declares this plat and dedicates to the use of the public forever all streets and avenues shown hereon, and the use thereof for all public purposes consistent with the use thereof for public highway purposes, together with the right to make all necessary slopes for cuts and fills upon the lots and blocks shown on this plat in the reasonable original grading of the streets and avenues shown hereon.

Plat Notes and Restrictions

1. An easement is hereby reserved under and upon the rear ten (10) feet of all lots and tracts in this plat for the installation and maintenance of utilities and drainage facilities, and the right to enter upon said lots at all times for said purposes. 2. No structure shall be erected nearer than twenty (20) feet to the front lot line of any lot. 3. No lot or portion of a lot in this plat shall be divided and sold, or resold, or ownership changed or transferred, whereby the ownership of any portion of this plat shall be less than the area shown on the face of this plat.
